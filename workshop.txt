description=Off-grid electricity for [b]Build 42[/b]: solar, wind, pedal, water, steam and gas, stored in battery banks and run from a charge controller. Light your base after the shutoff and wire whole houses back up.

description=[tr][td][b]Multiplayer[/b][/td][td]Built for it, not yet tested on a dedicated server[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Add mid-save[/b][/td][td]Yes. Saves from Off-Grid: Solar Power do not carry over[/td][/tr]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/first-system.png[/img]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Place a [b]solar array[/b] in the open, facing the sun.
description=[*]Place a [b]battery bank[/b] and load it with car batteries.
description=[*]Place a [b]charge controller[/b].
description=[*]Run a cable from each part to the controller (right-click, Connect), then switch it on.
description=[/olist]
description=The in-game [b]Dazed Mod Suite Guide[/b] (the book button on the left sidebar) walks through the rest.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-make.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/sources.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Solar arrays[/b], [b]tracking arrays[/b] that follow the sun (up to 15% more), and a 2x2 [b]Large Solar Array[/b]. Snow, dust and wear cut output.
description=[*][b]Windmills[/b] that turn to follow the wind, plus a windsock and weather vane to read it.
description=[*][b]Pedal generators[/b] you sit on and ride.
description=[*]A [b]micro-hydro wheel[/b] beside water: a steady 350 W, slower in ice.
description=[*][b]Steam engines[/b] burn fuel and drink water.
description=[*][b]Propane, petrol and wood gas generators[/b] start and stop on their own at the levels you set. Makeshift ones are pull-start. Wood gas runs on logs and planks, needs a few minutes to warm up and its grate cleaned of ash.
description=[*][b]Car alternator[/b]: a parked car with its engine running feeds up to 500 W.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-store.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/storage.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Battery banks[/b]: floor racks hold 3, 6 or 8 car batteries, wall boxes 2, 3 or 4. Cold lowers capacity, cycles wear cells, and a low-voltage disconnect protects them.
description=[*][b]Charge controllers[/b]: Makeshift manages 8 parts, Workshop 24.
description=[*][b]Load priority[/b]: Essential, Normal or Low per machine. As the batteries run down, Low is cut first.
description=[/list]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/board.png[/img]
description=Right-click the controller, [b]System monitor[/b]: the analog charge board shows sources and load on two dials, every cell, today's harvest, every circuit, the main isolator and your generators.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-gear.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/gear.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Transformers[/b] carry power across town and wire whole buildings through Dazed Core's [b]Building Picker[/b]: lights and appliances inside work off your batteries.
description=[*][b]Charger bench[/b] for loose batteries; any wired part charges a parked car within 10 tiles.
description=[*][b]Electric fence[/b] stuns zombies, never people. [b]Room cooler[/b] keeps a whole room's food like a fridge. [b]Space heater[/b] warms its room with Dazed Climate.
description=[*][b]Wall gauge[/b], [b]solar lamps[/b], and the [b]Knox County Almanac[/b]: read the sky for a month of weather.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-hazard.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/hazards.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Wiring a live system can burn your hand. Switch the controller off first.
description=[*]Thunderstorms can trip the isolator or damage the controller. A [b]grounding rod[/b] near the controller takes the strike.
description=[*]A Makeshift bank charged hard in a closed room builds up hydrogen. Open a window.
description=[*]A generator under a roof fills the building with fumes. Run it outside.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-tiers.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/tiers.png[/img]
description=Most parts come as [b]Makeshift[/b], [b]Salvaged[/b] or [b]Workshop[/b]. Learn them from the [i]Dazed Power Handbook[/i] and [i]Workshop Power Systems[/i], or find solar rigs on houses around Knox County. Electricians know every Makeshift build. Anything over 30 kg is carried as parts.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-works.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Dazed Plumbing[/b]: generators and steam engines feed from piped tanks; pumps, purifier, fuel pump and drilled well run off your controller.
description=[*][b]Dazed Climate[/b]: heater and cooler change room temperature; batteries and engines feel the cold.
description=[*][b]Dazed Cooking[/b]: the food dehydrator runs off your system.
description=[*][b]Dazed Essentials[/b]: bulk jobs leave powered machines alone.
description=[*][b]Electric Vehicles[/b]: charge homemade EVs from your banks.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Power/images/h-sandbox.png[/img]
description=14 options, from array output and battery wear to lightning, cable cost and found solar gear. Or pick one [b]preset[/b] (Easy, Standard, Realistic, Hardcore) on the Dazed Core page to set them all at once.

description=[*]Build 42 only.
description=[*]Do not run it alongside Off-Grid: Solar Power; Dazed Power replaces it.
description=[*][b]Dazed Dank[/b]: Dank gear works in a building Power has wired, but Dank does not register it as Power loads, so load priority skips it.
description=[*]Multiplayer has not been tested on a dedicated server yet. If something desyncs, please tell me.
description=[/list]
