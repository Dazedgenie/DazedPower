description=[h2]Your first system[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Place a [b]solar array[/b] in the open, facing the sun.
description=[*]Place a [b]battery bank[/b] and load it with car batteries.
description=[*]Place a [b]charge controller[/b].
description=[*]Run a cable from each part to the controller (right-click, Connect), then switch it on.
description=[/olist]
description=The controller's monitor shows what the system makes, stores and uses. The in-game [b]Dazed Mod Suite Guide[/b] (the book button on the left sidebar) walks through the rest.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Making power[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Solar arrays[/b] – single panels, [b]tracking arrays[/b] that turn to follow the sun (up to 15% more), and a [b]Large Solar Array[/b] of four panels on one 2x2 frame. Snow, dust and wear all cut output.
description=[*][b]Windmills[/b] that follow the wind, plus a windsock and weather vane to read it.
description=[*][b]Pedal generators[/b] – make power while you ride, with a proper pedalling animation.
description=[*][b]Water wheels[/b] beside a river or lake for steady power in any weather.
description=[*][b]Steam engines[/b] that burn fuel and drink water.
description=[*][b]Propane and petrol generators[/b] that start and stop on their own at the battery levels you set. Makeshift ones are pull-start.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Storing and running it[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Battery banks[/b] – floor racks hold 3, 6 or 8 car batteries, wall boxes 2, 3 or 4. Cold weather lowers capacity, every cycle wears a cell a little, and a low-voltage disconnect protects them.
description=[*][b]Charge controllers[/b] – Makeshift manages 8 parts, Workshop 24. The analog monitor has DAY, BATT, LOADS, SOURCES and GEN pages.
description=[*][b]Load priority[/b] – mark machines Low, Normal or Essential. As the batteries run down, Low is cut first.
description=[*][b]Transformers[/b] carry power across town and wire whole buildings with the Building Picker: lights and appliances inside work as if the grid were still on.
description=[*][b]Battery Charger Bench[/b] for loose batteries, and charge a parked car from any wired part within 10 tiles.
description=[*][b]Wall power gauge[/b], [b]electric fence[/b] (knocks down and stuns zombies, never hurts people), [b]room cooler[/b] that keeps a room's food like a fridge, and garden and street [b]solar lamps[/b].
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Hazards[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Wiring a live system can burn your hand. Switch the controller off first.
description=[*]Thunderstorms can trip the isolator or damage the controller. A grounding rod nearby takes the strike.
description=[*]A Makeshift bank charged hard in a closed room builds up hydrogen. Open a window.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Three tiers[/h2]
description=Most parts come as [b]Makeshift[/b], [b]Salvaged[/b] or [b]Workshop[/b]. Learn them from the [i]Dazed Power Handbook[/i] and [i]Workshop Power Systems[/i], or find ready-made solar rigs on houses around Knox County. Electricians start knowing every Makeshift build. Anything over 30 kg is carried as parts, like a vanilla bed.

description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=Array output, bank size, battery ageing, snow and dust build-up, lightning, hydrogen risk, cable cost, shock when wiring, link and transformer reach, found-rig chance, who may pick parts up, and a Realistic Mode with 1993 panels. Or pick one [b]preset[/b] (Easy, Standard, Realistic, Hardcore) on the Dazed Core page to set them all at once.
